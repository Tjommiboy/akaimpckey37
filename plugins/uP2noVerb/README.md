# uP2noVerb (v0.1.0, effect, uid `UQpo`)

A vintage-digital style reverb for the MPC, in the spirit of the hardware reverbs of the late 1970s
and 1980s. By uP2noGood. Original code (MIT). The tank follows the published "figure-8" allpass-loop
topology (J. Dattorro, JAES 1997); nothing is taken from any other product.

## Pages (one bank of four per column)

**VERB**: Main (Mix, Predelay, Decay, Output) | Type (Mode, Color, Size, Attack) |
Damping (High Freq, High Shelf, Bass Xover, Bass Mult) | Modulation (Mod Rate, Mod Depth)

**TONE**: EQ (High Cut, Low Cut) | Diffusion (Early Diff, Late Diff)

## Modes

| Mode | Character |
|---|---|
| Concert Hall | big, sparse start, lush chorus |
| Bright Hall | same family, brighter |
| Plate | dense and bright from the first moment |
| Room | small, quick to build |
| Chamber | medium, dense |
| Ambience | early reflections plus a short tail; Attack = early/late balance |
| Cathedral | very large, slow build, darker |
| Nonlin | gated burst; Size = length (80-580 ms), Attack tilts it towards reverse, Decay not used |

## Color

- **1970s**: dark (rolls off towards 10 kHz), deep and noisy modulation, soft clipping in the loop.
- **1980s**: full bandwidth, noisy modulation, soft clipping in the loop.
- **Now**: clean, gentle sine modulation.

## Controls

Decay is the mid-frequency decay time (0.2 to 70 s). Bass Mult makes the bass below Bass Xover decay
that many times as long. High Shelf turns the treble above High Freq down on every trip round the
loop. Size scales the room (0.4x to 1.6x of the mode's own size); turning it while sound is ringing
bends the pitch, as on the old hardware. Attack: low = the reverb starts at once, high = it swells.

## Honest notes

- Not yet tested on an MPC, and tuned by measurement only, not by ear. Measured on the desktop: decay
  times track the Decay knob (2.0 s set gives about 2.0-2.2 s), left and right are decorrelated, and
  Decay 70 s with Bass Mult 4x stays bounded in all three colors. Expect the mode tunings to need
  adjusting after listening.
- The eight modes are tunings of one network plus two special cases; they are not eight separate
  algorithms like the plugins that inspired this.
- The 1970s color is a filter and modulation character; the internal sample rate is not lowered.
- The skin is flat and follows the Color switch (three palettes). The palette change uses conditional
  visibility of the background image, which is untested on the MPC; if it does not switch, the 1980s
  palette stays on screen and everything still works.
- CPU on the desktop simulator is about twice uP2no2A; still light, but check it on the Key 37.

## Build

    python3 tools/build.py plugins/uP2noVerb
    .sdk-tools/host_sim plugins/uP2noVerb/build/native/up2noverb.dylib test --wav plugins/uP2noVerb/build/test.wav

The skin in `skins/` is generated by `skin_tools/make_skin.py` (needs Pillow; re-run it after changing
the layout, then rebuild). `--preview file.png` writes a picture of the pages in all three palettes.
